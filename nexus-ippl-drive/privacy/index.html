Privacy Policy

NEXUS IPPL Drive — Last updated 14 October 2026

Purpose and access

This private, single-owner desktop application uses Google OAuth to access Google Drive and upload or organize academic course files. The requested Google Drive scope allows access to files and folder metadata in the owner's account.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. Uploaded files are stored in the owner's Google Drive. The application does not intentionally transmit these credentials or files to a separate developer server, sell personal information, or use it for advertising. Google processes data as the Drive service provider.

Retention and control

The owner may revoke authorization at Google Account permissions, remove local OAuth credentials, or delete files from Google Drive. Revocation does not automatically delete uploaded files.

Contact

For questions, use the developer contact email displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
