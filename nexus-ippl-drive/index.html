NEXUS IPPL Drive

A private desktop application for organizing academic course materials in the owner's Google Drive account.

How it works

The owner authorizes access with Google OAuth. The utility uploads selected local course files to folders in the owner's Google Drive and checks for duplicates. It is not a public service.

Data and permissions

The application currently requests Google Drive access to work with an existing folder. Credentials remain on the owner's computer. Access may be revoked in Google Account settings.
